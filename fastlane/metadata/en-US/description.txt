Train your eye for color mixing, the way painters actually do it.

You get a goal color. Add parts of real paints from a painter's palette until your mix matches, then submit.

After every round you see:
• How close you got, as a match percentage
• Whether your mix is too light or dark, too dull or bright, and which way the hue is off
• The simplest recipe for the goal: the fewest paints and the ratio between them, like 3 Titanium White : 1 Cadmium Red

Mixing works like real pigment, not like a screen. Blue and yellow make green, yellow and black make olive, and white cools and dulls a color as it lightens it.

Pick your palette:
• Limited palette: titanium white, ivory black, cadmium yellow, cadmium red, ultramarine blue, yellow ochre and burnt umber
• Primaries: red, yellow, blue, white and black
• Zorn palette: white, black, yellow ochre and cadmium red

Pick your goals:
• Mixable colors you can always reach
• Named pigment and common colors like Cerulean, Burnt Sienna and Sky Blue
• Any color at all, to learn your palette's limits

Browse the color library to see every named color, grouped by hue and searchable, and practice any one of them.

Rounds at 90% or better add to your lifetime score. No accounts, no ads, nothing collected.
