# Color Match support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email [andybrochat@gmail.com](mailto:andybrochat@gmail.com).

**How do I play?** Look at the goal color, tap **+** on paints to add parts, and tap **Submit**.
You'll see your match percentage, hints on value, saturation and hue, and the fewest paints and
ratio that make the goal.

**Where is my score saved?** On your phone only. Deleting the app resets it.

**Why can't I match some colors?** With "Any hex code" goals, some colors are outside what your
palette can mix. That's part of learning a palette's limits.
